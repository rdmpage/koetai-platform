## Landmines worth remembering

- **SQLite table rebuild** (`migrate_backends_schema.py`): build the replacement
  under a *new* name and rename it INTO place. Do **not** rename the original out
  of the way first — modern SQLite rewrites child-table foreign keys to follow
  the rename, leaving them pointing at a table you then drop. Verify with
  `PRAGMA foreign_key_check` on a copy before touching the live DB.
- Always back up (DB / Caddyfile) to scratch and test schema/config changes on a
  **copy** first. Run `caddy validate` before every reload.
- Verify end-to-end by driving the real app/HTTP, not just imports.
